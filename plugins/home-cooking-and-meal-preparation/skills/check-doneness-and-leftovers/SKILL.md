# Check doneness and leftovers

The user measures, observes, stores, reheats, or discards food. Never report those physical actions as completed without confirmation.

## Establish the state

Ask for food type, thickest size, cooking method, location/jurisdiction, thermometer availability/reading, visible state, time at room temperature, intended serving/storage, and reheating equipment. Keep doneness and leftover questions separate when only one applies.

## Doneness decision

1. Prefer the current local food authority's food-specific internal-temperature and rest-time rule. Measure in the thickest central part without touching bone/container and clean the probe before/after use.
2. If no thermometer is available, provide only source-supported observable fallbacks and state their limitation. Examples include opaque/flaking fish, opened shellfish, set egg, boiling soup/stew, and clear/non-pink meat juices where local guidance uses those signs.
3. A Hong Kong CFS household baseline is a core temperature of at least 75°C for thorough cooking/reheating; other jurisdictions may use food-specific combinations. Do not merge conflicting tables into a fake universal number.
4. If evidence is uncertain, continue cooking safely or discard rather than guessing.

## Hold, cool, store, or reheat

- Serve promptly, or maintain safe hot/cold holding according to local guidance.
- Divide food into shallow/smaller clean containers for prompt cooling; do not leave perishables at room temperature beyond the applicable limit.
- Separate raw from cooked/ready-to-eat food and label storage date.
- Reheat thoroughly as cooking, not mere warming. Follow the local time/temperature rule and avoid repeated cooling/reheating cycles.
- Apply use-by dates and elapsed-time rules even when food looks or smells normal.

## Recovery and stop

For undercooked food, continue controlled cooking and recheck. For overcooking, protect safety first, then suggest serving/moisture adjustments without pretending damage is reversible. Discard food with unsafe time/temperature history, failed packaging, or strong spoilage signals.

Stop for allergic symptoms or suspected foodborne illness and seek medical help. For smoke, uncontrolled hot oil, flame, gas smell, or appliance fault, stop cooking and follow local emergency guidance; do not attempt hazardous repair.

Sources: [Hong Kong CFS cooking guidance](https://www.cfs.gov.hk/tc_chi/consumer_zone/safefood_all/five_keys_apply_cook.html) and [CFS storage/leftover guidance](https://www.cfs.gov.hk/english/consumer_zone/other_foodsafety/reduce_foodwaste.html).
